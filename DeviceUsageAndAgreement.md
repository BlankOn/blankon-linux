# Device Usage and Care Agreement

Recipients of computing devices provided by the BlankOn Foundation agree to the following terms:

1. **Personal and Professional Use**  
   The device may be used for personal purposes beyond BlankOn-related activities, including as a daily driver, for academic studies, professional work, and other personal needs. Such use is fully permitted.

2. **BlankOn Linux Requirement**  
   The device must run BlankOn Linux as its operating system.

3. **Device Care and Maintenance**  
   Recipients are expected to take reasonable care of the device and keep it in good working condition. Any issues, malfunctions, or physical damage should be reported to the Foundation promptly so that both parties can discuss and determine an appropriate solution.

4. **No Fixed Usage Period**  
   There is no fixed contract period or expiration date for using the device. Recipients may continue using it for as long as they wish, provided they remain actively involved in contributing to the BlankOn community.

5. **Avoid Permanent Modifications**  
   Recipients are encouraged to avoid applying stickers, labels, or other modifications that may leave permanent marks or damage the device's appearance. This helps keep the device in good condition and makes it easier to hand over to future contributors or the next generation of maintainers when the recipient eventually retires from their role.
